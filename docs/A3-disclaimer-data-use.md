## One-liner

A plain-language disclaimer and data-use notice for a ~10-user friends-and-family research tool — not a formal ToS/Privacy Policy, and not a public-launch legal package. Also folds in A8 (public-data sanity check), since at this scale it's one paragraph, not a separate signed-off checklist.

**Update (2026-07-19):** The product now generates explicit buy/sell/stop-loss price guidance in addition to a direction/confidence/rationale signal — a deliberate, conscious scope change, not a drift. This is a materially different, higher-stakes thing to hand someone than a directional research signal: it's a concrete instruction someone could act on with real money. The disclaimer below has been strengthened accordingly. This also means the original "nothing here is a recommendation to buy, sell, or hold" framing no longer describes the product honestly — it's been rewritten to be truthful about what the tool now actually outputs, rather than kept as a disclaimer that quietly stopped being accurate.

## Scale context

This product is capped at **~10 known friends-and-family users**, invite-only, free, no billing infrastructure. That scale is why this doc replaces what would otherwise be a drafted ToS + Privacy Policy + a separate regulatory sign-off checklist (A8) — see `TICKETS.md`'s Epic H/I/J notes for the same scale-down applied elsewhere.

## Disclaimer copy (shown at signup and on every prediction/signal card)

> **This is not financial advice, and this tool is not a licensed financial advisor.** Signals — including the direction, confidence score, and any buy/sell/stop-loss levels shown — are generated by an AI model based on public data and may be wrong, incomplete, or badly timed. Price levels are suggestions for your own consideration, not instructions, and they carry real risk of loss if you act on them. You are solely responsible for your own trading decisions and any resulting gains or losses — consult a licensed financial advisor before acting on anything you see here.

## Data-use notice (shown once, e.g. in onboarding or an "about the data" panel)

> This tool pulls price/chart data (Alpha Vantage/Twelve Data), financial news (Finnhub, with NewsAPI as fallback), public SEC insider filings (Form 4 via EDGAR), and public StockTwits posts to generate each signal. Social/forum content is unverified speculation, not fact, and is always labeled as such.
>
> StockTwits data is used via its public streams API, on the working assumption that a small, free, invite-only tool qualifies as non-commercial use. That assumption is tracked, not formally legally verified — see A2's risk notes — and will be re-checked if this product ever takes on billing or grows beyond friends and family. (Reddit was the original source; it was dropped on 2026-08-02 when Reddit closed self-serve API access. The assumption above is unchanged in substance and simply now attaches to StockTwits.)

## Public-data confirmation (folds in A8's regulatory boundary check)

All four ingested sources are public by construction, not by review:

- **SEC Form 4** — legally mandated public disclosure, pulled via SEC EDGAR's own public API.
- **Financial news** — public news APIs (Finnhub, NewsAPI).
- **Price/chart data** — public market data APIs (Alpha Vantage/Twelve Data).
- **Social/forum speculation** — public StockTwits posts, explicitly labeled "unverified sentiment," never presented as fact. Stored with an author-standing score that reflects follower count and account age, never accuracy.

There is no ingestion path (Epic B) for non-public, insider, or informally-obtained information — no formal sign-off checklist is needed at this scale; this paragraph *is* the check.

## Open item carried forward

Global signup with no geofencing is in scope (per A1 discussion) — this notice is written in plain English and assumes users read it in good faith, not as a jurisdiction-specific legal document. If this product ever moves beyond friends and family, this doc is the first thing to redo as a real ToS/Privacy Policy.
